**Data Science project: Forecasting the future competences of the state administration of Montenegro**

The Human Resources Administration should be able to answer several related questions: what competencies does the state administration need today, what will it need in five to ten years, how many employees will leave the system, what personnel should be hired and what competencies should be developed through training.

Main question: ***What number and types of employees, jobs and competencies will be needed by the state administration of Montenegro in the next 5-10 years, and how should employment, employee development and training be planned in order to match future needs with available staff?***
